Crew companion app · iOS

Layover is a duty-day companion for airline crew: a next-flight briefing, an EASA minimum-rest calculator, a layover city guide, a packing checklist, and a personal travel atlas — all in one place. It's a personal project, built to show what a focused, well-designed crew tool could look like.

Frequently asked

Is the flight and crew data real?

No — Layover currently ships with one fixed, fictional sample trip (Istanbul to London) so every screen has something real to show. It isn't connected to any airline's actual roster or flight data.

Does my checklist or crew list get saved?

Changes you make — ticking off a packing item, adding a crew contact — stay only for as long as the app is open. Closing and reopening Layover resets it to the sample state. Nothing is saved to a server or account.

Does it need an internet connection?

Only for one thing: the “Nearby the hotel” card on the City tab, which looks up pharmacies, ATMs, and shops around the hotel through Apple Maps. Everything else works fully offline. Without a connection that card simply shows no results.

Something isn't working — how do I report it?

Email a description of what happened (and a screenshot, if you have one) to the address below — it helps a lot.
