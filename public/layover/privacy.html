LAYOVER

Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Layover is a personal iOS app built by Alperen Şamil İlmaz. This policy explains, as plainly as possible, what the app does and doesn't do with your data. Short version: it doesn't collect any, and the only thing it ever sends anywhere is a place search to Apple Maps.

Layover does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you see runs on your device. The single exception is the “Nearby the hotel” card, which asks Apple Maps for places around the layover hotel — see below.

What the app doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in of any kind
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos
  • No internet use of its own — apart from the Apple Maps place search described below, the app works fully offline

Nearby places search (Apple Maps)

On the City tab, the “Nearby the hotel” card can look up practical places — pharmacies, ATMs, supermarkets, malls, and shops — around the layover hotel. This uses Apple's MapKit, so the search is sent to Apple Maps. What's sent is the category you chose (for example “Pharmacy”) and the fixed coordinates of the sample hotel. It does not use your device's location, and Layover never asks for location permission. The app doesn't attach any account, identifier, or other personal data to the request, and nothing from the search is saved on the device or anywhere else. Apple's handling of Maps requests is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

What's on screen

The flight, crew, and city details you see in Layover are fictional sample data, bundled with the app. Anything you add or check off yourself — a packing item, a crew contact — is kept only in memory while the app is open, and is cleared the next time you launch it. None of it ever leaves your device.

Children's privacy

Layover does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, for the simple reason that it doesn't collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the date above will reflect that. As of today, nothing is collected.
